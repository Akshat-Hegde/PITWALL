Setup guide

This guide is for ${cafe}. Your installer is made for your cafe: it already holds your licence for ${rigs_text} and your cafe's ${prefilled}. Follow these steps in order.

1. What you need

  • One hub PC. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), connected to the router with a cable, and switched on whenever the cafe is open. It does not need the internet. Every rig sends its data to this PC, and the TV, the staff page and the guests' phones get their pages from it.
  • Your ${rigs_text}, each with ${game} installed, on the same router as the hub PC.
  • A TV connected to the hub PC or to another PC in the cafe.
  • A USB stick for the backups (step ${backup_step}).

Install ${product} on one PC only. Another PC that only drives a TV gets the "TV only" install (step ${tv_step}).

2. Fix the addresses in your router

Every rig is told the hub PC's address, so it must never change. In your router's admin page, find DHCP reservation (some routers call it "static lease" or "address reservation") and reserve the current address of the hub PC and of each of your ${rigs_text}.

3. Run the installer on the hub PC

  1. Copy ${installer} to the hub PC and double-click it.
  2. If a blue window says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. If Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?", click Yes.
  3. Choose Hub PC. Leave Keep this PC awake ticked. Tick Open the TV page when this PC signs in if this PC also drives the TV.
  4. On the last page, write down the setup code. It is shown once, in large type. If you lose it, use the Start-menu shortcut ${product} Show setup code.
  5. Leave Open the setup page ticked and click Finish.

If Windows Security or another antivirus blocks ${product}, contact ${supplier}.

4. The setup page and your PINs

Enter the setup code. ${prefilled_check}

Your hub address is shown on the setup page. Write it down: you type it on every rig (step 5).

Then choose two PINs (4 to 8 digits each):

  • Owner PIN: opens the owner page and the setup page. Only the owner should know it.
  • Staff PIN: opens the staff page, which staff use every day. Make it different from the owner PIN.
  • Don't use easy PINs like 1234, 0000 or a birthday. Keep a copy somewhere safe, away from the counter.
  • Forgot the owner PIN? On the hub PC, use the Start-menu shortcut ${product} Reset owner PIN. Laps, rigs and settings are kept.

${wifi_step}. Test the guest Wi-Fi from a phone

Guests' phones must be on a Wi-Fi that can reach the hub PC. ${wifi_text}

  1. On the setup page (or the staff page's Health tab), find Test from your phone.
  2. Join the guest Wi-Fi on a phone and scan the QR code with its camera.
  3. The phone should say ${phone_ok}

If the page doesn't open on the phone, guests can't check in or open their driver cards. Turn off "guest isolation" (sometimes "AP isolation") in the router, or use your main Wi-Fi.

${tv_step}. The TV

TV on the hub PC: use the Start-menu shortcut ${product} TV, or tick "Open the TV page when this PC signs in" in the installer.

TV on another PC: run the same installer on that PC and choose This PC only shows the TV. Type the hub address from the setup page.

${backup_step}. Backups to a USB stick

Every night the hub saves a backup of your laps, settings and rig names. Keep them on a USB stick that stays plugged into the hub PC, so they survive if the PC breaks:

  1. Plug the USB stick into the hub PC and note its drive letter (for example E:).
  2. Open the owner page (Start-menu shortcut ${product} Owner) and find the Backups card.
  3. Type a folder on the stick, for example E:\Backups, and press Use this folder. Then press Back up now.

Backups hold guests' phone numbers and the Wi-Fi password: keep the stick safe.

${licence_step}. Your licence

Your licence covers ${rigs_text} and ${games}. It is yours forever: there is nothing to renew. The licence code is on your licence certificate, and the installer has already put it in.

To add rigs, games or features later, contact ${supplier}. You get a new code: on the owner page, find the Licence card, paste it into the Enter new code box and press Use this code. It works at once, with no reinstall. Racing is never stopped: a rig beyond your licence still records laps, but isn't shown on the TV.
