    Cafe
    Opening hours
    Sessions still open at closing time end automatically, and their cards are made.
    Open all day and night (24 hours) isn't supported yet: close at 06:00 the next day at the latest.
    PINs
    Staff use the staff PIN on the staff page; the owner PIN opens the owner and setup pages. The staff PIN may be the same as the owner PIN.
    Guest Wi-Fi (optional)
    Shown as a QR code on the TV. Use the network guests' phones join: it must reach this PC (test it below).
    Test from your phone

    Join the guest Wi-Fi on a phone, then scan this code with its camera.

    Rigs
    On every rig, in F1 25: Game Options › Settings › UDP Telemetry Settings: UDP Telemetry On, UDP Broadcast Mode Off, UDP IP Address = , UDP Port , UDP Send Rate 20Hz, UDP Format 2025. Your Telemetry may stay Restricted.
    This includes a rig on this PC: use the address above, not 127.0.0.1.
    Reserve this address for the hub PC in your router (DHCP reservation), or rigs lose the hub when the router restarts.

    A rig appears here once it sends telemetry. Not sure which rig is which? Drive on one rig: the rig whose car is moving right now says Moving now. Names show on the TV (up to 16 characters).

    If a rig PC gets a new address it shows up as a new rig. On the new one choose "Same rig as…" and its old entry: the name, colour, number and check-in sticker move over.

    Rig addresses and changes need the owner PIN.
